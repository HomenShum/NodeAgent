Why this shape

NodeAgent is a career, compiled into four surfaces

Each capability is the production form of a problem I already spent years solving. The agent is the part that finally makes them one loop.

Banking · Finance

Pressure-testing models

↓ becomes
Years where a wrong assumption was a tracked, defensible change — never a silent overwrite — became NodeAgent's versioned spreadsheet engine. Every edit is a delta with a version bump and an audit log.
→ spreadsheet/applySpreadsheetDelta.ts
Data Engineering

Context ingestion → structure

↓ becomes
Pipelines that turned messy, multi-source inputs into structured truth became NodeAgent's context collector and 4-layer grounded search — retrieval confidence, claim grounding, citation chains, the right doc for the right answer.
→ chat/contextCollector.ts · search/searchAndSynthesize.ts
Agentic AI

Orchestration & eval

↓ becomes
Tool orchestration, schemas, and eval discipline became the runtime that drives the room, the search, the model, and the memo as a single reliable loop — bounded, honest about failure, traceable end to end.
→ node-agent/runtime/nodeAgentRuntime.ts
Not vapor

The four surfaces are real, ported modules

Distilled from the NodeBench AI monorepo into clean, testable TypeScript. This page is the no-build proof; the repo is the contract.
